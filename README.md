# swiggy-food-delivery-data-analysis
Data analysis project exploring Swiggy food delivery trends using Python. The project analyzes sales patterns, restaurant ratings, pricing, and customer preferences across multiple cities in India.
## Project Overview

This project analyzes a large Swiggy food delivery dataset to understand customer ordering behavior, restaurant performance, cuisine demand, and pricing patterns across different cities in India. The objective of the analysis is to extract meaningful insights that can help food delivery platforms improve customer engagement, restaurant partnerships, and operational efficiency.

Using Python-based data analysis techniques, the dataset was cleaned, explored, and visualized to identify trends in ratings, popular cuisines, city-wise order distribution, and price ranges.

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains more than **197,000 records** of food delivery information including restaurant details, food categories, pricing, and customer ratings.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following key performance metrics were calculated to evaluate overall platform performance:

### 1. What is the Total Sales generated from all orders?
This KPI measures the total revenue generated from all food orders in the dataset.

### 2. What is the Average Rating given by customers?
This KPI helps understand overall customer satisfaction with restaurants and food quality.

### 3. What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
Average Order Value indicates the average amount spent per order and helps measure customer purchasing behavior.

### 4. What is the Total Rating Count?
This KPI represents the total number of customer reviews and ratings, which indicates customer engagement.

### 5. What is the Total Number of Orders?
This KPI measures the total volume of food orders placed on the platform.

## Key Business Insights

### Top Revenue Drivers
Highly rated restaurants (4.5⭐ and above) contribute a significant portion of total revenue, indicating that customer trust and ratings strongly influence purchasing decisions.

**Recommendation:** Introduce a Premium Partner Program to provide high-performing restaurants with increased visibility and promotional benefits.

### Cuisine Demand
North Indian and Chinese cuisines dominate the overall order volume, while healthy and salad options receive higher ratings but appear less frequently in the dataset.

**Recommendation:** Encourage restaurants to expand healthy food offerings in high-demand locations.

### Peak Ordering Hours
Order activity is highest between **7 PM and 10 PM**, especially during weekends.

**Recommendation:** Introduce flash deals and discounts during **off-peak hours (3 PM – 6 PM)** to improve demand distribution.

### Customer Loyalty
Frequent users tend to prefer **free delivery offers and combo deals**.

**Recommendation:** Enhance subscription programs with loyalty rewards and milestone incentives.

### Operational Efficiency
Cities with high restaurant density show a relatively lower average order value due to higher competition.

**Recommendation:** Use cross-selling strategies and bundle offers to increase average order value.
